What Happens Next

1
Within 2 hours

A consultant will contact you

A senior consultant from our team will call or email you to introduce themselves, confirm your details, and book your briefing call. Check your inbox - including your spam folder.

2
Briefing call - 20-30 min

You tell us what happened

We'll ask you the key questions about your situation. You don't need to prepare anything - just be ready to tell us your story. We do the rest.

3
Within 1 business day of your briefing

We prepare and lodge your application

We draft your Fair Work Commission application in full and lodge it on your behalf. You'll receive a copy once submitted.

We prepare you thoroughly, attend with you, and represent you throughout. Most matters resolve here. We push hard for the best outcome.
