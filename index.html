Common Questions

Straight answers.
No spin.

Do I need a lawyer to go to the Fair Work Commission?
No. The Fair Work Act explicitly allows parties to be represented by non-lawyers, including HR professionals and industrial relations consultants. In many cases, non-lawyer representation is just as — or more — effective than legal representation.
What is the deadline for an unfair dismissal claim?
21 days from the date of your dismissal taking effect. Missing this deadline means your application will almost certainly be rejected. If you're close to the limit, contact us immediately — we can act fast.
What happens at conciliation?
Conciliation is a confidential telephone or video conference facilitated by the Commission. A conciliator helps both sides negotiate. The vast majority of Fair Work matters resolve at this stage. We'll prepare you and represent you throughout.
What if my matter doesn't settle at conciliation?
If you're on the Full Representation package, we continue with you to the formal hearing. If you started on Lodge & Conciliate, you can upgrade to Full Representation at a set top-up fee.
Who do you represent?
We represent employees only. If you've been unfairly dismissed, had your workplace rights violated, experienced bullying or harassment, or need support through a Fair Work Commission process — we're in your corner.
Are all fees really fixed? No hidden costs?
Yes. What we quote is what you pay for our representation. There are some Commission filing fees payable directly to the Fair Work Commission (currently $79.80 for most applications, waived for some concession holders) which are separate from our fees.
